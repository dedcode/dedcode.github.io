About Me

I am the Computer Science Program Head and an Assistant Professor of Computer Science at NYU Abu Dhabi, and a Global Network Assistant Professor of Computer Science at NYU’s Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences.

My research combines data and knowledge engineering with machine learning to address challenges arising from incomplete and heterogeneous data. My work spans knowledge representation and completion, question answering and graph reasoning, multimodal learning, and explanation of model predictions. I place particular emphasis on developing interpretable reasoning systems that can support human-machine collaboration for knowledge completion and curation.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Fribourg, Switzerland, and my M.S. from the University of Louisiana at Lafayette as a Fulbright Scholar. Previously, I was a Research Scientist at the Wikimedia Foundation and a Data Science Fellow at NYU’s Center for Data Science. I also held internships at Microsoft Research and through Google Summer of Code.
