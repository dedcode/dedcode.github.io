3. Trace the ROC curve

A positive crossing moves up; a negative crossing moves right. Tied scores cross together. The dashed diagonal is the random-ranking reference.

Area under the complete curve:

AUC = (correctly ordered positive–negative pairs + ½ × tied pairs) / all positive–negative pairs.

The faint line shows the complete curve. AUC summarizes all thresholds, not just the current one.

Scores are synthetic ranking scores, not necessarily calibrated probabilities. Each example contains 10 instances of each class. AUC measures ranking; it does not choose a threshold or account for the cost of mistakes.
